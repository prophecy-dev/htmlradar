Golden Journey Deck

The fixture the golden journeys upload. Three sections, three headings, nothing clever — the point is that the numbers it produces are predictable enough to compare two runs of it.

What this deck is for

A recipient opens this deck, passes the email gate and reads each section for a few seconds. Everything the product then records — the session, the section dwell, the notification, the events — is what the parity record captures.

Why three sections

Three is the smallest number that proves the tracker distinguishes sections at all rather than crediting one block for the whole visit. More sections would only make the run longer without proving anything the third one does not.

What a failure here means

If this deck stops producing three section rows, or the dwell stops landing near the time the journey actually spent, something in the recipient path changed. The design overhaul is not supposed to touch that path at all.
